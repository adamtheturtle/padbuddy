## Description

Pads for CoderPad brings your CoderPad account to iPhone and iPad. Browse and search interview pads and questions, inspect pad details, and create pads without returning to your desk.

Pads for CoderPad is an unofficial, third-party app. It requires an existing CoderPad account and your own CoderPad API key. It is free to download.

FEATURES

• Browse pads — Find pads with search and filters for state, language, owner, team, type, and date.
• Pad details — Review code, session events, participants, and interview metadata.
• Create pads — Start a pad and seed it from a saved question.
• Questions — Browse and search your question bank and mark favorites for quick access.
• Multiple accounts — Switch between CoderPad accounts, including self-hosted or regional deployments.
• Made for mobile — An adaptive interface for iPhone and iPad that supports light and dark appearance.

PRIVACY

Your API key is stored as a synchronizable Apple Keychain item, so Apple may sync it across your trusted devices when iCloud Keychain is enabled. The app otherwise uses it only to talk to your configured CoderPad API. The app collects no analytics, shows no ads, and has no developer-operated server. See the privacy policy for details.
