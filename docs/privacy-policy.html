Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-12

This privacy policy describes how the PadBuddy macOS app ("the app") handles your information. The app is an unofficial, third-party client for CoderPad; it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by CoderPad, Inc.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no developer-operated server. The app talks only to CoderPad's own API, using an API key that you supply.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your CoderPad API key is stored only in the local macOS Keychain on your Mac. It never leaves your device except in requests sent directly to CoderPad's API to fetch and manage your own data.
  • App preferences and a local cache (selected account, last-viewed section, saved searches, favorite questions, and a cache of pads/questions so the app opens quickly) are stored locally on your Mac. None of this is uploaded anywhere by the app.

What the app sends, and to whom

When you use the app, it makes requests directly from your Mac to CoderPad's API (https://app.coderpad.io, or a self-hosted/regional URL if you configure one) to read and manage the pads, questions, organization, and quota your CoderPad account already has access to. Those requests are authenticated with the API key you provide. CoderPad's own handling of that data is governed by CoderPad's privacy policy, not this one.

Data collection

The app collects no data on behalf of the developer. Its bundled PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy manifest declares no tracking, no tracking domains, and no collected data types.

Children

The app contains no objectionable content and is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Your control

You can remove your API key and all locally stored data at any time by removing the account in the app's Settings, or by deleting the app. There are no developer-side accounts to delete, because the app never creates any.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@adamdangoor.com or raised on the project's issue tracker.
